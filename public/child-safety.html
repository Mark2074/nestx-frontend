Child Safety Standards

NestX policy for child safety, abuse prevention, reporting, and enforcement.
NestX is intended for users aged 18 and over.

NestX has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of minors, grooming, or any content involving minors in sexual or exploitative contexts.

Reporting

Users can report accounts, posts, media, or other content that may violate our safety rules. Reports are reviewed and may result in content removal, account restrictions, account suspension, or escalation where required.

Moderation and Enforcement

Content suspected to involve minors, exploitation, abuse, or illegal activity may be removed or restricted. Accounts involved in such activity may be suspended or permanently removed.

Cooperation with Authorities

Where legally required or appropriate, NestX may preserve relevant records and cooperate with law enforcement or competent authorities.

Contact

Child safety and abuse prevention contact: safety@nestx.live.
