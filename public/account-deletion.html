Account Deletion

Information for NestX users about deleting an account and related data handling.

Users can permanently delete their NestX account directly from the application settings or profile section.

When an account deletion request is submitted, the account enters a pending deletion state for up to 30 days.

Data Deleted

  • Profile information
  • Posts and uploaded content associated with the account
  • Followers and following relationships
  • Personal account preferences and settings

Data That May Be Retained Temporarily

  • Security logs
  • Moderation and abuse-prevention records
  • Technical records required for legal compliance and fraud prevention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal obligations, platform security, fraud prevention, abuse investigations, or enforcement of platform rules.

Privacy Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact privacy@nestx.live.
